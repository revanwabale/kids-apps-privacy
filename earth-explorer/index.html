Earth Explorer: Kids Atlas — Privacy Policy

No data collected

Last updated: 2 July 2026

Earth Explorer: Kids Atlas is an educational globe-exploration game for children (ages 4–10): spin a beautiful 3D planet, meet continents, countries, capitals and famous cities, and play the Find It! game. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information whatsoever.

What we collect

Nothing. Earth Explorer: Kids Atlas has:

  • No accounts, sign-in, or registration.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs or services.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.
  • No network connection — the app works fully offline.

What stays on the device

Game progress and settings (and, where the app asks for it, an optional first name used only for friendly greetings) are saved only on the device itself. This information never leaves the device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is removed if the app is deleted. Progress can also be reset any time from the app's grown-ups area, which sits behind a press-and-hold parental gate.

Children's privacy

Earth Explorer: Kids Atlas is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids Category requirements: because we collect no data at all, there is nothing to disclose, request consent for, or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
